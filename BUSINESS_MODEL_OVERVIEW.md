# OPEV / FloatMaps Business Model, Capital & Structure Overview

## Revenue Model

**Primary Streams:**
1. **Energy Sales (BaaS)**: Charge per kWh drawn from OPEV modular batteries (net consumption only).
2. **Subscription Tiers**: Monthly fee for advanced features (ghost racing, priority kiosk access, analytics, gifting tools).
3. **Grid Services**: Revenue from discharging unused packs back to the grid (peak shaving, frequency regulation, emergency reserves).
4. **Hardware & Licensing**: Potential licensing of modular interface / kiosk tech to OEMs.
5. **Data & Insights**: Anonymized mobility + energy data (with consent).

**Gifting Economics**: Gifted energy is transferred as credit. Original user is not charged for gifted portion. System can take a small facilitation fee or use it to drive retention/network effects.

## Startup Capital Requirements (High-Level)
- **Hardware**: Kiosk manufacturing + initial battery inventory (major capex).
- **Software & Platform**: FloatMaps development, backend, integrations (VESC, FloatControl, etc.).
- **Deployment**: Site acquisition, installation, permitting.
- **Working Capital**: Operations, support, initial marketing.
- **R&D**: Cell balancing systems, grid-tie inverters, 360/3D pipeline.

Phased approach recommended: Pilot in Greenville → regional rollout → national.

## Organizational Structure
- Likely Delaware C-Corp for investor-friendliness.
- Founder (Robert Metta Thomas) retains significant equity.
- Early team: Engineering (hardware + software), Operations, Partnerships (utilities/cities).
- Future rounds: Seed → Series A focused on scaling kiosks and grid partnerships.

## Intellectual Property Protection
- File provisional patents on:
  - Modular battery interface & dynamic voltage assembly
  - Kiosk architecture with cell balancing + grid feedback
  - Gifting/transfer protocol for returned energy
  - Integration methods with third-party apps (VESC, FloatControl)
- Trade secrets on optimization algorithms (balancing, routing, predictive inventory).
- Copyright on FloatMaps software and UI.
- Consider defensive publications for certain methods.

This combination of recurring energy revenue + grid services + strong IP creates a defensible, high-margin infrastructure business.
