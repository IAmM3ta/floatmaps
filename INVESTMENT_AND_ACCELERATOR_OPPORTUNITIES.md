# Other Investment & Startup Incubator Opportunities

## Top-Tier Accelerators (Hardware/Infrastructure Friendly)
- **Y Combinator** (already primary target) - Strong for ambitious founders with clear vision.
- **Techstars** (various programs, including mobility/climate tracks)
- **MassChallenge** - Good for hardware and deep tech; non-dilutive elements in some cohorts.
- **Plug and Play** - Strong in mobility, energy, and hardware startups.
- **Village Global** or **Bolt** - Hardware and deep tech focused.

## Climate Tech & Infrastructure Focused
- **Breakthrough Energy Fellows** (Bill Gates) - Excellent for energy and grid-related ideas.
- **Clean Energy Ventures** or similar climate funds/accelerators.
- **Third Derivative (D3)** - Climate tech accelerator.
- **Greentown Labs** - Hardware/climate hardware focused incubator (Boston area).

## Mobility & Hardware Focused
- **Highway1** (PCH) - Hardware accelerator.
- **HAX** (Shenzhen-based hardware accelerator, now part of PCH).
- **Starters** or mobility-specific programs.

## Angel Groups & Pre-Seed Funds
- Look for angels with hardware, energy, or mobility experience.
- Regional groups near South Carolina or Southeast US.
- Climate-focused angel syndicates (e.g., via AngelList or Climate Angels networks).

## Strategic / Corporate
- Utility companies or energy companies with innovation arms (for grid angle).
- EV/PEV related corporates (potential strategic investment or partnership).
- Automotive or micromobility companies interested in infrastructure layer.

## Non-Dilutive / Grants
- DOE / ARPA-E related programs (if grid angle is strong).
- SBIR/STTR grants for hardware or energy tech.
- State-level economic development grants (South Carolina).

## Strategy Recommendation
1. Prioritize programs with hardware or climate/infrastructure focus.
2. Use the pilot plan and grid services angle to stand out.
3. Warm intros remain the highest-leverage activity.
4. Apply broadly but tailor narrative to each program's thesis (e.g., emphasize grid for climate programs, community for general accelerators).
