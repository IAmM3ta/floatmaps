# Sample Financial Projections (Conservative Scenario)

## Key Assumptions
- Pilot starts with 10 kiosks in Year 1
- Average net energy consumption per active user: 2.5 kWh/month from OPEV packs
- Energy price: $0.55 / kWh
- Subscription conversion: 20% by end of Year 2
- Grid services revenue starts meaningfully in Year 2

## Year 1 (Pilot Phase)

| Metric                  | Amount          | Notes                          |
|-------------------------|-----------------|--------------------------------|
| Active Users (EOY)      | 1,200           | Pilot users                    |
| Energy Revenue          | $198,000        | Net kWh sold                   |
| Subscription Revenue    | $28,800         | 200 paying users @ $12/mo      |
| Grid Services           | $15,000         | Early pilot contracts          |
| **Total Revenue**       | **$241,800**    |                                |
| Operating Expenses      | $850,000        | Team, ops, marketing           |
| Hardware Depreciation   | $180,000        | Kiosks + batteries             |
| **EBITDA**              | **($788,200)**  | Investment year                |
