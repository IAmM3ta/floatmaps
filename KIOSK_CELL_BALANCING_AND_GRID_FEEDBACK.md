# Kiosk-Level Cell Balancing + Grid Feedback Value Proposition

## Kiosk-Level Cell Balancing

Instead of balancing only at the individual pack level, the kiosk can actively balance across **all modular packs** in its inventory:

- Smart BMS communication between packs
- Dynamic charge redistribution
- Improved pack longevity and safety
- Higher effective usable capacity

This is a significant operational advantage over traditional shared-fleet models.

## Grid Feedback / Vehicle-to-Grid (V2G) Capability

**Unused but fully charged modular packs** sitting in the kiosk can be discharged back into the local energy grid.

**Benefits:**
- **Grid balancing & peak shaving** for utilities
- **Emergency backup power** for cities and first responders
- **Revenue stream** for OPEV (selling energy back during high-demand periods)
- **Investor appeal**: Turns every kiosk into a distributed energy storage asset

**Investor Pitch Angles:**
- City governments: Resilient microgrid + emergency power
- Utility providers: Distributed storage without capex on new batteries
- Emergency services: Reliable backup during outages
- Overall: OPEV kiosks become **active grid participants**, not just consumers

## Technical Path
- Bidirectional inverters in kiosks
- Smart communication with utility APIs or local microgrid controllers
- Prioritization logic (reserve minimum packs for riders vs grid discharge)

This significantly strengthens the OPEV investment case beyond pure micromobility.
