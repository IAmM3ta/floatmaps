## Slide 2: The Problem
- ~15 million personal electric vehicle (PEV) owners in the US alone.
- FAA lithium battery rules make it nearly impossible to fly with devices (Onewheels, EUCs, e-scooters, e-bikes, e-skateboards).
- Current options are painful: expensive/specialty shipping or leaving the device behind.
- Existing players (closed hardware like Future Motion, shared fleets like Bird/Lime) do not solve the core problem.
- Result: Riders are grounded. The freedom they bought is limited.

## Slide 3: Our Solution
**OPEV (Open-Source Personal Electric Vehicles)**
- Rider owns the vehicle.
- OPEV owns and manages the energy through a network of intelligent modular battery kiosks.

**FloatMaps**
- The community platform that makes the system magical:
  - 3D globe to detailed topo maps
  - Ghost racing & personal records
  - Device integration & custom tunings that transfer at pickup
  - Community gifting of leftover charge
  - Hardware integrations (VESC, FloatControl, etc.)

**Simple User Flow**: Fly with empty board → Pick up charged modular pack at destination → Ride freely → Return or gift remaining charge.

## Slide 4: Market Opportunity
- Large, passionate, and fast-growing PEV community.
- North American installed base: 15–21 million personally owned devices.
- Removing the travel barrier dramatically expands the addressable market and usage.
- Additional upside from grid services as kiosk network scales.

## Slide 5: Product & Key Features
- Seamless 3D Globe → Local Topo Map experience
- Ghost racing against your previous bests
- PEV device lookup + custom tunings synced to modular batteries
- Community gifting of returned energy (with QR code)
- Hardware integrations with popular apps (VESC, FloatControl, Floaty)
- Future: 360 camera TrailView + 3D reconstruction

## Slide 6: Business Model
**Multiple high-margin revenue streams:**
- Energy sales (net kWh from OPEV modular batteries)
- FloatMaps Pro subscriptions
- Grid services (discharging unused packs to the grid)
- Hardware & technology licensing

Gifting mechanics drive retention and network effects while maintaining fair billing.

## Slide 7: Technology & Defensibility

**Defensibility**: Patents + trade secrets on optimization, balancing, and integration methods.
