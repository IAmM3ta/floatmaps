# OPEV / FloatMaps - Starter Pitch Deck Outline

## Slide 1: Title
- OPEV + FloatMaps
- The Infrastructure Layer for Personal Electric Vehicles
- Founder: Robert 'Metta' Thomas

## Slide 2: The Problem
- 15M+ PEV owners in the US alone cannot travel with their devices due to FAA lithium battery restrictions.
- Devices are either shipped (expensive, risky) or left behind.
- Existing solutions (closed hardware, shared fleets) fail riders.

## Slide 3: The Solution
- OPEV: Rider owns the vehicle; OPEV owns and manages the energy via modular battery kiosks.
- FloatMaps: The community platform (mapping, ghost racing, gifting, device integration).
- Seamless travel: Fly with empty board, pick up charged modular pack at destination.

## Slide 4: Market Opportunity
- Large and fast-growing PEV market (Onewheels, EUCs, e-bikes, e-scooters, e-skateboards).
- North American installed base: 15-21M personally owned devices.
- TAM expansion through removal of travel barrier.

## Slide 5: Product & Features
- 3D Globe → Topo Map experience
- Ghost racing & personal bests
- Device lookup + custom tunings that transfer at kiosk
- Community gifting of leftover charge
- Hardware integrations (VESC, FloatControl, etc.)
- Future: 360 TrailView + 3D reconstruction

## Slide 6: Business Model
- Energy sales (net kWh from modular batteries)
- Subscriptions (FloatMaps Pro)
- Grid services (discharging unused packs)
- Hardware licensing

## Slide 7: Technology & IP

- Grid feedback capability
- Gifting & transfer protocol
- Strong IP moat + trade secrets
