# Detailed Financial Model Spreadsheet Outline

## Sheet 1: Assumptions
- User growth assumptions (monthly active users, kiosk density)
- Average daily/ride energy consumption from OPEV packs
- Subscription conversion rate and churn
- Average revenue per user (ARPU) from energy + subscriptions
- Grid services utilization rate per kiosk
- Hardware deployment costs per kiosk
- Operating costs per kiosk (power, maintenance, site fees)
- Gross margin targets on energy sales
- Customer acquisition cost (CAC)
- Funding assumptions and dilution

## Sheet 2: Revenue Build
- Energy Sales: # of active users × avg kWh per user × price per kWh
- Subscription Revenue: Paying users × monthly fee
- Grid Services: # of kiosks × utilization × revenue per kWh discharged
- Other (licensing, data)
- Monthly/Quarterly/Annual roll-up

## Sheet 3: Costs
- COGS (energy procurement, battery degradation, kiosk power)
- Operating Expenses:
  - Hardware maintenance & replacement
  - Software & cloud costs
  - Personnel (engineering, operations, sales)
  - Marketing & customer acquisition
  - Site/permitting costs
- Capex schedule (kiosk & battery purchases)

## Sheet 4: P&L Projection (3-5 years)
- Revenue lines
- Gross Profit
- Operating Expenses
- EBITDA
- Depreciation
- Interest & Taxes
- Net Income
- Key metrics: Gross Margin %, EBITDA Margin, etc.

## Sheet 5: Cash Flow
- Operating Cash Flow
- Investing Cash Flow (capex)
- Financing Cash Flow
- Ending Cash Position
- Runway calculation

## Sheet 6: Balance Sheet (simplified)
- Assets (cash, inventory, fixed assets)
- Liabilities
- Equity

## Sheet 7: Unit Economics & KPIs
- LTV (Lifetime Value)
- CAC payback period
- Contribution margin per user
- Energy margin per kiosk
- Grid services margin
- Churn rate
- Net revenue retention

## Sheet 8: Scenario Analysis
- Base / Upside / Downside cases
- Sensitivity on key variables (energy price, kiosk utilization, subscription conversion)
- Break-even analysis

## Sheet 9: Funding & Cap Table
- Seed / Series A assumptions
- Use of funds
- Post-money valuation scenarios
- Founder & early employee equity
