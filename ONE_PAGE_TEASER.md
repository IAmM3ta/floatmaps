# OPEV + FloatMaps - One Page Teaser

**The Problem**
15+ million personal electric vehicle owners in the US cannot travel with their devices because of FAA lithium battery restrictions. Current solutions (closed hardware, shared fleets) leave riders grounded.

**Our Solution**
OPEV builds the missing infrastructure: modular battery kiosks where riders own their vehicles and OPEV manages the energy. FloatMaps is the community platform (mapping, ghost racing, device integration, gifting).

**Key Innovation**
- Fly with empty board, pick up charged modular pack at destination.
- Custom tunings transfer automatically at kiosk pickup.
- Community gifting of leftover charge via QR.
- Kiosks with cell balancing that can also feed energy back to the grid.

**Business Model**
- Energy sales (net kWh)
- Subscriptions
- Grid services revenue

**Why Now & Why Us**
Massive PEV adoption + clear travel pain point + strong IP moat on modular systems and kiosk intelligence. Founder Robert 'Metta' Thomas has built the full technical architecture and working demo.

**The Ask**
Raising to deploy first kiosks and run Greenville pilot. Building the infrastructure layer that makes personal electric vehicles truly free.
