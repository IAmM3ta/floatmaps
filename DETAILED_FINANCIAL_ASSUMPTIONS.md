# More Detailed Financial Assumptions

## User & Market Assumptions
- Initial target: PEV enthusiasts in pilot city (Greenville) + early adopters nationally
- Average active user rides 8–12 times per month
- Average energy draw from OPEV modular battery per ride: 1.8 – 3.2 kWh (depending on device and riding style)
- % of rides using OPEV batteries vs personal charging: 65% in Year 2, growing to 80%+
- Subscription conversion rate: 18–25% of active users by end of Year 2
- Monthly churn rate: 4–6%

## Pricing Assumptions
- Energy price to user: $0.45 – $0.65 per kWh (competitive with home charging + convenience premium)
- FloatMaps Pro subscription: $12.99/month or $129/year
- Grid services revenue per kiosk (mature): $800 – $2,500 per year (depending on location and utility contracts)

## Cost Assumptions
- Average kiosk deployment cost: $45k – $75k (including initial battery inventory)
- Annual operating cost per kiosk: $8k – $14k (power, maintenance, site fees, insurance)
- Battery replacement / refresh cycle: Every 3–5 years depending on usage and chemistry
- Software & platform opex: Scales with users but relatively fixed per kiosk

## Growth Assumptions
- Pilot (Year 1): 8–15 kiosks
- Year 2: 40–80 kiosks (regional expansion)
- Year 3: 150–300 kiosks
- User growth tied to kiosk density + word-of-mouth + gifting network effects

## Key Metrics Targets
- Gross margin on energy: 60–70% once kiosks are mature
- Contribution margin per kiosk (energy only): Positive within 12–18 months
- LTV:CAC target: >3x by Year 2
- Payback period on kiosk: 24–36 months

These assumptions can be adjusted in the financial model spreadsheet.
