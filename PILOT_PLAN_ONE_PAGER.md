# OPEV + FloatMaps Pilot Plan - One Pager

**Greenville Pilot (Minimal Viable Pilot)**

## Objective
Prove demand, operational feasibility, and net energy accounting for the OPEV modular battery + FloatMaps system with the least capital required. Generate credible data and testimonials to support larger funding rounds and utility partnerships.

## Scope
- **Location**: Greenville, SC metro (strong existing PEV community)
- **Kiosks**: 10 kiosks (phased deployment)
- **Users**: Target 800–1,500 active users
- **Duration**: 12 months

## Phased Timeline

**Months 0–2: Foundation**
- Finalize kiosk design & suppliers
- Secure first sites & begin permitting
- Complete backend + gifting/return flows
- Recruit initial user cohort

**Months 3–5: Soft Launch**
- Deploy first 5 kiosks
- Onboard users & test full feature set
- Begin utility conversations

**Months 6–9: Scale & Operate**
- Expand to 10 kiosks
- Run at full scale (gifting, device integration, ghost racing)
- Track net energy consumption & billing

**Months 10–12: Analyze & Prepare**
- Analyze data & unit economics
- Document results for investors
- Explore expansion & grid partnerships

## Budget Estimate
**Total: $1.3M – $1.85M**
- Hardware & batteries: $750k – $1.05M
- Software & platform: $250k – $350k
- Operations & working capital: $300k – $450k

## Success Metrics
- >800 active users
- Strong kiosk utilization (>60%)
- High return compliance
- Accurate net energy tracking
- Gifting adoption >15%
- Early utility interest / MOU

## Why This Pilot Matters
This is the minimum credible step to de-risk hardware operations, prove the community + energy model, and open doors to grid services revenue — the key long-term differentiator.

**Next Step**: Execute this pilot to generate the traction needed for meaningful investment capital.
