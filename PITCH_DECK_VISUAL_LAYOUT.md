# OPEV + FloatMaps Pitch Deck - Visual Slide-by-Slide Layout Suggestions

## General Design Notes
- Clean, modern, dark theme with cyan (#67f6ff) accents (matching FloatMaps branding).
- Minimal text, strong visuals.
- Consistent iconography and typography.
- Use high-quality renders of the FloatMaps demo, kiosk concepts, and maps.
- Keep slides uncluttered — aim for 5-7 key points max per slide.

## Slide 1: Title
**Layout:**
- Top: Large logo + tagline
- Center: Main title + subtitle
- Bottom: Founder name + date
**Visuals:** Clean hero image of 3D globe transitioning to map or kiosk concept.

## Slide 2: Executive Summary
**Layout:**
- Top: One-sentence hook
- Middle: 4-5 bullet points in two columns
- Bottom: Key metrics or vision statement
**Visuals:** Simple icons for Problem / Solution / Model / Vision.

## Slide 3: The Problem
**Layout:**
- Left side: Strong visual (grounded rider or FAA restriction graphic)
- Right side: 4-5 clear pain points with icons
**Visuals:** Before/after or frustrated rider imagery.

## Slide 4: Competitor Landscape
**Layout:**
- Top: 2x2 or 3-column competitor matrix
- Bottom: Our differentiation callouts
**Visuals:** Competitor logos + simple comparison table or radar chart.

## Slide 5: The Solution
**Layout:**
- Top: Simple user journey flow (Fly → Pickup → Ride → Return/Gift)
- Bottom: Key product pillars with icons
**Visuals:** Clean flowchart + product screenshots from the demo.

## Slide 6: Market Opportunity
**Layout:**
- Left: TAM/SAM/SOM graphic
- Right: Key stats + growth drivers
**Visuals:** Large numbers, clean charts.

## Slide 7: Product & Key Features
**Layout:**
- Grid of 4-6 feature cards with icons + short descriptions
**Visuals:** Screenshots from FloatMaps demo (globe, ghost racing, device lookup, gifting).

## Slide 8: Business Model
**Layout:**
- Top: Revenue streams as cards or icons
- Bottom: Simple unit economics or margin highlights
**Visuals:** Icons for Energy, Subscriptions, Grid Services.

## Slide 9: Technology & Defensibility
**Layout:**
- Left: Core technology pillars
- Right: IP moat visualization (layers or fortress metaphor)
**Visuals:** Technical diagrams or abstract moat imagery.

## Slide 10: Traction & Roadmap
**Layout:**
- Top: Current traction (demo, backend, pilot plan)
- Bottom: Timeline roadmap
**Visuals:** Clean timeline graphic.

## Slide 11: Team
**Layout:**
- Large photo of founder
- Short bio + key strengths
- Optional: Advisors or early team if available
**Visuals:** Professional headshot + clean bio layout.

## Slide 12: The Ask
**Layout:**
- Top: Clear ask amount + use of funds breakdown (pie or bars)
- Bottom: Contact + next steps
**Visuals:** Simple use-of-funds chart.
