# Maximizing Likelihood of Raising Capital - Action Plan & Narrative

## Core Positioning (Recommended Narrative)

**OPEV + FloatMaps is building the infrastructure layer that makes personal electric vehicles truly usable at scale.**

We solve the #1 friction preventing widespread adoption and daily use: the inability to travel with your device. At the same time, our kiosk network creates a new category of distributed energy assets that can participate in grid services.

This is not just another hardware or app play. It is **infrastructure that serves both riders and the grid**.

## Key Narrative Adjustments to Maximize Investor Interest

1. **Lead with Capital Efficiency**
   - Phase 1: Software + community layer (FloatMaps) + backend to prove demand and unit economics with minimal hardware.
   - Phase 2: Pilot 8-15 kiosks in one city (Greenville) to validate operations and grid angle.
   - Phase 3: Scale with grid partnerships de-risking further capital needs.

2. **Emphasize Grid Services Early**
   - Kiosks are not just cost centers — they are potential revenue-generating grid assets.
   - This opens doors to climate tech, infrastructure, and utility-adjacent investors.

3. **Defensibility & Moat**
   - Modular battery interface + kiosk intelligence (balancing + grid feedback)
   - Gifting protocol and community network effects
   - Integrations with existing hardware ecosystems

4. **Team & Founder Story**
   - Deep domain obsession + hands-on execution (demo + architecture already built)
   - Polymath founder who can bridge hardware, software, and systems

## Recommended Investor Targets (Priority Order)

**Tier 1 (Highest Fit)**
- Climate tech / decarbonization funds
- Infrastructure / energy transition VCs
- Mobility + hardware-tolerant funds

**Tier 2**
- Angels with hardware or energy experience
- Regional funds near pilot city

**Tier 3**
- Generalist VCs (only with warm intros)

## Immediate Actions to Improve Odds

1. **Pilot Plan** (High Priority)
   - Define minimal viable pilot (number of kiosks, timeline, success metrics)
   - Identify potential site partners in Greenville
   - Create simple pilot budget and timeline

2. **Materials**
   - Updated pitch deck with capital efficiency + grid narrative
   - One-pager / teaser
   - Financial model (use outlines already created)

3. **IP**
   - File provisional patent(s) on core inventions (modular interface, kiosk balancing + grid feedback, gifting protocol)

4. **Network**
   - Warm intros to climate/infra investors
   - Reach out to PEV community leaders for pilot support / testimonials

5. **Team**
   - Consider adding technical advisor or co-founder with hardware/operations experience

This plan focuses on de-risking the biggest concerns (capital intensity and execution) while playing to the unique strengths of the idea.
